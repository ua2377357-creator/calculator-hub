How it works

Frequently asked questions

What is EMI?

Equated Monthly Installment — the fixed monthly payment that covers interest plus principal.

Does a longer loan save money?

No. EMI goes down, but total interest goes up.

Is this EMI exact?

It is the standard formula. Your bank may add fees, taxes or a slightly different rate.
