How it works

Example: bill 2,000, tip 15% → tip 300, total 2,300. Split among 4 → 575 each. In the USA 15–20% is common for good service; check if service charge is already added.

Frequently asked questions

What is a normal tip?

In the USA 15–20% is common. In many other countries tipping is smaller or not expected.

Should I tip on tax?

Most people tip on the pre-tax bill, but either way is accepted.
